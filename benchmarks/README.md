These are the benchmark problems used in the CG-iLAO* journal paper.

* The problems in `kloessner21_adapted` were taken from [https://zenodo.org/records/4604720](https://zenodo.org/records/4604720), and adapted to work with our code
* The PARC printer problems in `probabilistic-parc-printer` are from ["Occupation Measure Heuristics for Probabilistic Planning" ICAPS'17](https://felipe.trevizan.org/papers/trevizan17:hpom.pdf)
* The intermediate tireworld problems in `intermediate-triangle-tireworld` are from the [CG-iLAO* AAAI'24 paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14636)
